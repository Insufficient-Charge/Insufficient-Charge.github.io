About This Portfolio

Design Rationale

The primary goal of this portfolio is to truly represent who I am. Pure technical achievements and metrics matter, but finding the right team and culture requires understanding the person behind the systems.

From taking GPU hardware enablement from early silicon integration into fleet production at Google Cloud, to leading technical communities, writing fiction, and running tabletop worlds—systems engineering and storytelling share the same creative core: solving complex problems and communicating ideas clearly.

"We are dreamers, shapers, singers, and makers. We study the mysteries of laser and circuit, crystal and scanner, holographic demons and invocations of equations. These are the tools we employ, and we know many things."

∼ Elric, Babylon 5
