Beyond the Tech

Community & Leadership

I am passionate about bringing people together and fostering collaborative spaces. At Google, I led the Seattle cohort of the early-career network ("Noograds"), organizing regular meetups and professional events for over 100 engineers.

During university, I served as President of the Society of Software Engineers, directing workshops for 200+ students, and led the 20+ member committee for Concinnity 24, a major annual gaming and sci-fi convention.

Writing

I am a passionate fiction writer! I've written multiple short stories, several of which have won short-story competitions, and I am actively developing novel and audio narrative projects.

Writing keeps my imagination sharp, hones my communication skills, and informs how I approach narrative structure and user experience in software. Feel free to ask me about my publications!

Escape Rooms

I have an analytical obsession with escape rooms, boasting a 95% lifetime success rate across dozens of rooms.

While at Google, I organized the intern and team "Escape Room League". In Seattle and during my travels, I actively target top completion times and leaderboard records with fellow puzzle-solvers.

Tabletop & Game Mastering

In university, I served as Vice President and Interim President of MAGE (a 400+ student club dedicated to sci-fi, anime, and gaming). I've also served as an external playtester for board game designers in early prototyping stages.

As a long-time TTRPG Game Master, my background in creative writing and systems design allows me to craft immersive, dynamic worlds and tactical encounters that players love.

eSports

I was invited to join the collegiate Overwatch eSports team at my university, where we practiced regularly, competed in division matches, and earned 3rd place regionally against other universities.
